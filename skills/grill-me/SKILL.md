# Grill Me

Use this whenever GTM evidence needs interpretation.

## Ask Format

```text
Question:

My current best hypothesis:

Why this matters:

What changes depending on your answer:
```

## Ask Before

- Changing positioning, ICP, value-prop hierarchy, tone/taste, proof claims, or major strategy.
- Explaining why a customer bought or churned.
- Promoting a campaign result into a lesson.
- Deciding which customer proof is externally usable.
- Resolving conflicting source evidence.

## Do Not Ask For

- Routine file routing.
- Formatting.
- Source facts that can be checked with connected tools.

## Rule

Tools answer what happened. The operator or accountable owner decides what it means.
