# GTM Brain Section Map

Use this compact structure unless the company has a strong reason to expand it.

```text
README.md
AGENTS.md
company/
icp/
messaging/
market/
brand/
sales/
customers/
systems/
projects/
skills/
```

## Canonical Sections

- `company/`: what the company is, what it does, product truth, claims, tone foundation.
- `icp/`: best customers, ICP, personas, segments, disqualifiers, qualification rules.
- `messaging/`: buyer language, objections, value props, positioning, offers.
- `market/`: category, competitors, alternatives, market context.
- `brand/`: brandkit, design-system brief, visual rules, Claude.ai/design resources, speaker-tone profiles.
- `sales/`: campaign memory, outbound copy guide, campaign process, results, examples.
- `customers/`: customer list, proof approval, case-study notes, quotes, feedback, top customers.
- `systems/`: sources, tools, automations, update rules, lifecycle stages, metrics.
- `projects/`: unpublished workspaces by owner or project.
- `skills/`: reusable agent/operator workflows.

## Control Files

- `systems/mvp-checklist.md`: current build phase, explicit non-goals, completion criteria, and next checklist.
- `projects/{operator}/building-the-brain/README.md` or `session-relay/README.md`: durable handoff state for future sessions.

## Update Levels

- `Direct edit allowed`: factual fixes, links, tool status, obvious routing, formatting.
- `Propose update`: campaign learnings, buyer language patterns, objections, customer notes, proof candidates.
- `Owner review required`: positioning, ICP, value-prop hierarchy, public proof claims, tone/taste, customer interpretation, major strategy.
